THE CRAFT

Every piece is built
specifically for your project

Mr. Goodwood is a custom woodworking shop serving homeowners across the greater Rochester area who want something a big-box cabinet company can't give them: a piece built specifically for their space, by the person who actually builds it, then installed on site.

Kitchens, built-ins, closets, even one-of-a-kind furniture — every project starts with the room itself, not a catalog page.

  • Every project designed around your actual space
  • Real joinery and solid materials — not particleboard flat-pack
  • One craftsman, start to finish — no subcontracted labor
